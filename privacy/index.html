Movista — Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026

1. Overview

Movista is an iOS app that helps you track the movies and shows you have watched, build a watchlist, and discover new titles through AI-powered recommendations and friend suggestions. This policy explains what personal information Movista collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. We collect only what is necessary to provide the service and do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and a small amount of data generated automatically by the app.

Information you provide:

  • Email address — required for account creation; must be verified before you can log in.
  • Username — required, unique, minimum 3 characters.
  • Display name — required.
  • Profile photo — optional; selected by you using the iOS Photos picker. We access only the single image you choose.
  • Bio — optional short freeform text.
  • Diary entries — movie and show title references, personal ratings (multi-dimensional scales), and watch dates. Diary entries are private to your account by default. The exception is entries you rate highly (a "loved" rating): those may appear to your friends as a Story, a short-lived highlight of titles you loved. You control this by how you rate — lower ratings are never shown to friends.
  • Watchlist — references to movies you want to watch.
  • Streaming platform preferences — which services you subscribe to, selected from a preset list.
  • Friend graph — user IDs of accepted friends and pending requests.
  • Group membership — user IDs of shared watch groups you belong to.
  • Movie suggestions — suggestions sent between friends contain a movie ID and sender/recipient IDs only; no freeform message text.

Information collected through device permissions you grant:

  • Approximate location — with your permission, Movista uses your device's approximate location once, at signup, to fill in your city. We request reduced-accuracy location only, never your exact position, and we store just the resulting city name — not coordinates, not a location history, and no ongoing tracking.
  • Contacts — with your permission, Movista checks which of your phone contacts are already on the app so you can find and connect with people you know. Your contact list is matched securely (via a one-way hashing process) and is never stored on our servers in readable form.
  • Speech recognition (share extension only) — when you share a video link into Movista from another app, on-device speech recognition may be used to help identify which film or show it is. This processing happens entirely on your device; audio and transcripts are never stored or sent to our servers.

Information collected automatically:

  • Firebase User ID — a unique identifier automatically assigned to your account by Firebase Authentication.
  • App Check attestation token — a device attestation token generated by Apple's App Attest framework to verify that requests originate from a genuine instance of the Movista app on a real device. This token is used solely for request authenticity verification and is not stored long-term.
  • Daily AI usage counters — numeric counters that track how many AI requests you have made today, used to enforce per-user rate limits. Counters reset at midnight UTC.
  • Aggregated taste profile — a summary derived from your own ratings, used to generate personalized AI recommendations. This is not a separate data collection; it is computed from data you have already provided.
  • Compatibility scores — when you and a friend have both rated overlapping titles, we compute a taste-compatibility score between your two accounts. This is derived entirely from ratings you and your friend have already provided — no new data is collected to produce it.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics — device model, OS version, app version, and, if the app crashes, a stack trace of the code that was running. Used only to find and fix bugs — see Section 4 (Firebase Crashlytics / Performance Monitoring).

Information we do not collect:

  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) or cross-app tracking.
  • No precise/continuous location — approximate, one-time, city-level only, as described above.
  • No access to your photo library beyond the single profile photo you select, and no camera or microphone access.
  • No health, financial, payment, or biometric data.
  • No third-party advertising or marketing-analytics SDKs (no Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or equivalent).

Note: Movista also has a group chat feature in active development. It is not enabled in the current version of the app — no chat messages, photos, videos, or audio can currently be sent, and the app does not request camera or microphone access. This policy will be updated, and the relevant permissions re-added to the app, before that feature is turned on in a future release.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Account and authentication — your email, username, and display name identify you and allow you to log in.
  • Core features — your diary, watchlist, and ratings power the tracking, compatibility scoring, and friend-suggestion features.
  • AI recommendations — your aggregated taste profile and any prompt you type are sent to the Claude API to generate movie recommendations. See Section 4 for how this is handled.
  • Rate limiting — daily AI counters prevent abuse of the AI recommendation feature.
  • Friend and group features — your friend graph and group membership enable the social features of the app.
  • Location (optional) — your city is used to show local features such as people near you and what's in theaters nearby.
  • Contacts (optional) — used only to suggest friends who are already on Movista; matching happens via a one-way hash, and your contact list is never stored on our servers.
  • Speech recognition (share extension only) — used only to help identify which film or show is in a video you share into the app; audio and transcripts are processed on-device and never stored.
  • Content moderation — reports you submit are used to review and act on policy violations.
  • Service integrity — App Check tokens verify that API requests come from the genuine app.

4. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services receive data as part of operating Movista.

Firebase (Google LLC) — Firebase hosts all user data through its Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, and App Check products. All data described in Section 2 is stored in Firebase infrastructure. Firebase Privacy Policy.

Anthropic (Claude API) — When you request AI recommendations, your aggregated taste profile and any prompt you type are sent to the Claude API. Your email address, username, profile photo, and friend relationships are never sent. Requests are routed through a Firebase Cloud Function proxy; no identifying headers leave our infrastructure. Anthropic Privacy Policy.

The Movie Database (TMDB) — Movie search terms and lookup IDs are sent to TMDB to retrieve metadata such as titles, posters, and ratings. No personal data is sent to TMDB. TMDB Privacy Policy.

OMDb — Movie and show lookup IDs are sent to OMDb to retrieve Rotten Tomatoes scores. No personal data is sent to OMDb.

JustWatch — Movie and show lookup IDs are sent to JustWatch to retrieve links showing where a title is available to stream. No personal data is sent to JustWatch. JustWatch Privacy Policy.

Firebase Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring (Google LLC) — Movista uses these Firebase products to automatically collect crash reports and app performance data (device model, OS version, app version, and, if a crash occurs, a stack trace of the code that was running). This data is used only to find and fix bugs and slow spots in the app; it is not used for advertising or combined with your profile information. Firebase Privacy Policy.

Gmail / Google LLC (SMTP) — When a user submits a content report, a notification email is sent to the developer at mazani.movista@gmail.com via Gmail SMTP. No user data beyond what is included in the submitted report is transmitted through this channel.

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts — data is retained for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted accounts — data is erased immediately when you delete your account. Residual copies in backups are purged within 30 days.
  • Content reports — retained for 12 months after resolution (resolved means the report was dismissed, the content was removed, or the reported account was actioned), then automatically deleted.
  • Daily AI counters — reset at midnight UTC each day.

6. Your Rights

Account deletion — you can delete your account at any time in-app at Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion permanently removes your diary, watchlist, ratings, taste profile, groups you created, and all references to you in other users' friend lists, group member lists, and pending requests.

Data access — you may request a copy of your stored data by emailing mazani.movista@gmail.com.

Data correction — most user-editable fields (display name, bio, profile photo, streaming preferences, and ratings) can be updated directly in the app.

EU and UK residents (GDPR) — you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, and object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, email mazani.movista@gmail.com.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA) — you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. Movista does not sell or share personal information. To exercise other rights, email mazani.movista@gmail.com.

7. Blocking, Reporting, and Content Moderation

Movista provides in-app tools to block other users and to report users or groups that violate our guidelines. When you submit a report, the report document (including your user ID, the reported user or group ID, the selected reason, and any optional note you provide) is stored in Firestore and triggers a notification to the developer. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours in accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 1.2.

Blocking a user removes the friendship between you, hides each user from the other's friend search results and suggestions, and prevents further interaction within the app. Block relationships are stored server-side and enforced by Firestore security rules.

8. Children's Privacy

Movista is rated 12+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will remove the account and all associated data. Parents or guardians who believe their child has created an account may contact us at mazani.movista@gmail.com to request removal.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of the page. For material changes, we will notify users through the app or by email. Continued use of Movista after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise any of your rights, please email us at mazani.movista@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.
